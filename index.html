Random Process Learning Guide

Resource policy: keep the Random Process course path on MIT OpenCourseWare as much as possible. The main sequence is MIT 6.041 → MIT 6.262 → MIT 18.642. All three are public and free, and all official links stay on ocw.mit.edu. When MIT does not have a strong match, the guide uses Textbook-first instead of forcing an unrelated course.
Main resource order: ① MIT 6.041 for probability prerequisites; ② MIT 6.262 as the main stochastic-process course for Poisson, renewal, Markov and martingales; ③ MIT 18.642 for Brownian motion, stochastic integration and Black–Scholes. If the English in MIT 6.262 is difficult to follow, use Bilibili search for MIT 6.262 bilingual / Chinese-subtitle mirrors as a language backup, while keeping MIT OCW as the primary stable source.

Bayesian Statistics Learning Guide

Resource policy: Bayesian self-study is now designed to stay on Bilibili as much as possible. All resources below are public and free to watch. The main backbone is the bilingual Ben Lambert — A Student's Guide to Bayesian Statistics. Chinese university lectures are used where they match the textbook more closely. The goal is to avoid switching between many websites while still following 《贝叶斯统计及其R实现（第2版）》 section by section.
Main resource order: ① Ben Lambert 双语字幕课程：概念、共轭先验、模型比较、MCMC 主线； ② 西安交通大学梅长林《贝叶斯统计》：先验确定、估计、检验、预测； ③ 仅在决策理论等少数缺口处使用同一 Bilibili 网站内的补充视频。
