Random Process Learning Guide

Resource policy: keep the Random Process course path on MIT OpenCourseWare as much as possible. The main sequence is MIT 6.041 → MIT 6.262 → MIT 18.642. All three are public and free, and all official links stay on ocw.mit.edu. When MIT does not have a strong match, the guide uses Textbook-first instead of forcing an unrelated course.
Main resource order: ① MIT 6.041 for probability prerequisites; ② MIT 6.262 as the main stochastic-process course for Poisson, renewal, Markov and martingales; ③ MIT 18.642 for Brownian motion, stochastic integration and Black–Scholes. If the English in MIT 6.262 is difficult to follow, use Bilibili search for MIT 6.262 bilingual / Chinese-subtitle mirrors as a language backup, while keeping MIT OCW as the primary stable source.
Section Textbook Topic Primary Resource How to Use
1.1 概率空间 MIT 6.041 — Lectures 1–3 Probability models, axioms, conditioning, Bayes' rule and independence. Quick review is enough if these are already familiar.
1.2 随机变量与分布函数 MIT 6.041 — Lectures 5–10 Discrete and continuous random variables, PMF/PDF/CDF, joint distributions and derived distributions.
1.3 数字特征、矩母函数与特征函数 MIT 6.041 — Lectures 5, 7, 11–12 Use MIT for expectation, variance, covariance and conditional expectation. Textbook-first for MGF and characteristic functions because MIT 6.041 does not cover them in the same depth.
1.4 收敛性 MIT 6.262 — Lecture 3
MIT 6.041 — Lecture 19 		Start with 6.041 for convergence in probability and the weak law; use 6.262 for the stochastic-process viewpoint.
1.5 独立性与条件期望 MIT 6.041 — Lectures 3, 7, 12 Focus on independence and iterated conditional expectation. These ideas return later in Markov chains and martingales.
2.1 随机过程基本概念 MIT 6.262 — Lectures 1–2 Use Lecture 1 for the course-level stochastic-process viewpoint and Lecture 2 for the Bernoulli process as the first concrete process.
2.2 有限维分布与 Kolmogorov 定理 Textbook-first MIT 6.262 does not give a sufficiently direct treatment of the Kolmogorov extension theorem. Learn the theorem from the textbook and class notes rather than switching websites for one theorem.
2.3 随机过程的基本类型 MIT 18.S096 — Lecture 8 Useful mainly for stationary processes and time-series intuition. Use the textbook for the full classification used in your course.
3.1 Poisson 过程 MIT 6.262 — Lecture 4 Main Poisson-process lecture. Treat this as the primary resource rather than switching between multiple courses.
3.2 与 Poisson 过程相关的若干分布 MIT 6.262 — Lecture 5 Interarrival times, splitting, combining and conditioning. Pair the lecture with the textbook examples.

MIT 6.262 — Lecture 12 		Renewal rewards, stopping trials and Wald-type arguments. Use the textbook for the exact renewal-equation notation.

4.4 更新过程的推广 MIT 6.262 — Lecture 16 Useful for extensions and for the transition from renewal processes to countable-state Markov chains.

5.4 Markov 链的应用 MIT 6.262 — Lecture 9 Expected first-passage time, rewards and dynamic programming. Good application-oriented lecture.
